# Code of Conduct

This is a small project, so the short version: be decent to people.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about snow science or pass
  rosters, and reasonable people land in different places on both.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This weighting overstates elevation" is
  useful. "You clearly don't ski" is not.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline a change. A rejected PR is a scoping
  decision, not a verdict on you.
- Be patient. This is nobody's day job.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, including about someone's
  identity, background, or level of experience.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith argument.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other space
where you are participating in this project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, close issues and PRs, and
block accounts, with or without warning depending on severity.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ray-cj-huang), or use GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
flow if you would rather not go through the maintainer. Reports will be handled
as privately as the situation allows.

This document is written in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which is worth
adopting verbatim on a project larger than this one.
